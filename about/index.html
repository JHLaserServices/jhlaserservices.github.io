Meet Justina.

I'm Justina, a registered nurse with 20+ years' experience in the NHS. I've always had a passion for skincare and skin health, so opening a nurse-led laser clinic in Maidstone was a natural next step.

I offer IPL, laser and scar therapy treatments with personal, one-to-one care. I take time to listen and make sure you feel supported, from your first consultation to your final session.
