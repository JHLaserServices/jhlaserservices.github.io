PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED OCTOBER 2026

Your privacy.

Your information is treated with the same care as your treatment. This policy explains what I collect, why, and the choices you have.

WHO

Hissett Medical Ltd, trading as JH Laser Services, looks after your information.

WHAT

Your contact details, and the health information I need to treat you safely.

SHARING

Never sold. Only shared when needed to run the clinic, or when the law requires it.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

01

Who I am

JH Laser Services is a trading name of Hissett Medical Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales (company number 09377388). You can view its registered details on Companies House.

Hissett Medical Ltd is the controller of your personal information and is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number ZC289839.

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email hello@jhlaserservices.co.uk.

02

What I collect

  • When you get in touch by email, phone, text or Instagram message: your name, contact details and anything you include in your message.
  • If you book a consultation or treatment: your date of birth, medical history, medications, skin type, patch test results, signed consent forms and treatment notes.
  • Before and after photos, taken as part of your treatment record so I can track your progress. Photos are only ever used for marketing if you give separate written consent.
  • Payment records, such as what you paid and when. I don't keep card details.

You don't have to share your medical history, but if you'd rather not, I may not be able to treat you safely.

03

How I use your data

I only use your information where the law allows it. Below is each way I use it, with the legal reason that applies (known as the lawful basis).

Replying to you and arranging appointments. When you get in touch, I use your details to reply and book your appointment. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, as this is a normal part of running the clinic.

Assessing and treating you. I use your medical history to check a treatment is suitable for you and to carry it out safely. Lawful basis: contract, as I need this information to provide the treatment you've booked. Health information is special category data, so I also rely on it being used to provide health care, by a registered nurse bound by a duty of confidentiality (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(h)).

Keeping records. I keep clinical notes, accounts and insurance records. Lawful basis: legal obligation for financial records, which the law requires me to keep. For clinical records, legitimate interests, as my professional standards require them and they protect us both if there's ever a question about your care.

Photos for marketing. I only use before and after photos on the website or social media if you've given separate written consent. Lawful basis: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

I don't use your information to make automated decisions about you.

04

Who I share it with

I never sell your information. I only share it when I need to:

  • with trusted providers that help run the clinic, such as email, secure storage and my accountant, who may only use it on my instructions;
  • with my insurer, regulator or legal advisers if a claim, complaint or professional matter requires it;
  • with your GP or another healthcare professional, with your permission or in an emergency;
  • when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, it's protected by safeguards approved under UK law.

05

How long I keep it

Clinical records, including consent forms and photos, are kept for 8 years after your last treatment. This follows the NHS Records Management Code of Practice, which sets 8 years for adult health records.

Financial records are kept for 6 years from the end of the financial year they relate to, as HMRC requires of limited companies.

Enquiries that don't lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months. UK GDPR says information should only be kept for as long as it's needed.

Your information is stored securely, and your records are kept confidential.

06

This website

This website has no contact form, and doesn't use cookies or analytics to track you.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and its fonts are provided by Google Fonts. Like most websites, these services may log your IP address when you visit, to deliver the page and keep it secure.

Links to Instagram and Google Maps take you to their own sites, where their privacy policies apply.

07

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted or restricted where the law allows. You can also object to how it's used, ask to move it to another provider, or withdraw consent you've given, such as for marketing photos.

Some clinical records must be kept for the full retention period, even if you ask for them to be deleted. I'll always explain if that applies.

To make a request, email hello@jhlaserservices.co.uk. It's free, and I'll respond within one month.

08

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, please contact me first. I'll acknowledge your complaint within one month and do everything I can to put it right.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Questions about your information?

Email and I'll be happy to explain how it's looked after.
