HOW IT WORKS

Targeting the root, not the surface.

Light is absorbed by the pigment in each hair and heats the follicle that produces new growth. Hair grows in cycles, so treatment is a course of sessions spaced weeks apart, with regrowth becoming finer and sparser over time.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST SESSION
  • Shave the day before. Don't wax, pluck or use hair-removal cream during your course.
  • Avoid sun, sunbeds and fake tan for two weeks before.
  • Tell me about any medication, as some make skin more sensitive to light.
03 · QUESTIONS

IPL hair removal questions.

They're closely related. A laser uses a single wavelength of light, while IPL uses a filtered range of light. Both heat the follicle to reduce regrowth over a course of sessions.

Most people describe a quick snap against the skin, like a rubber band. Settings are adjusted to keep you comfortable.

It works best on darker hair. Very light, grey, white or red hair usually responds poorly. Your consultation will confirm whether it's right for you.

Most people see a long-term reduction after a full course. Hormonal changes can trigger new growth, so some people have an occasional top-up session.
